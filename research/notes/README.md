# Research Notes

This directory contains learning notes and technical understanding documents produced during the research phase of the project.

## What Belongs Here

- Notes on papers, blog posts, and technical resources that inform the project.
- Technical explanations of concepts encountered during research (e.g., evaluation metrics, retrieval methods, hallucination taxonomies).
- Design decisions that emerged from research and need to be recorded before implementation.
- Summaries of field-specific terminology and how it applies to this project.

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Random or stream-of-consciousness notes with no lasting value.
- Generated output from LLMs that has not been critically reviewed.
- Implementation code (use `src/` or `learning/` for that).
- Experiment configurations or results (use `research/experiments/` for that).

## Naming Convention

Name files descriptively with a date prefix where relevant:

```
YYYY-MM-DD_topic_name.md
topic_name.md
```

Keep notes concise, technically precise, and written for a future reader who needs to understand a decision or concept, not reproduce your thought process.
